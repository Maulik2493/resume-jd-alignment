# Problem Statement

## The Problem

Most resume tools today fall into one of two traps:

1. **ATS gaming tools** — They rewrite resumes to match keyword density targets,
   injecting buzzwords, inflating claims, and producing generic output that sounds
   like every other candidate. The candidate's real voice disappears.

2. **AI resume rewriters** — They take a resume and a JD, then freely hallucinate
   experience, invent bullet points, and produce a document the candidate cannot
   honestly stand behind.

Both approaches treat the resume as something to be *manufactured* rather than
*understood*. Neither helps the candidate know where they actually stand.

## What's Missing

There is no tool that simply answers the honest question:

> "Given my real experience, how well do I align with this specific role —
> and what can I realistically improve?"

Candidates need a system that:

- Reads their resume and a target JD
- Tells them exactly where they match and where they don't
- Clearly separates what can be improved through better wording (without lying)
  from what requires real experience they don't yet have
- Never invents, inflates, or rewrites on their behalf

## The Two Categories

This is the core insight of the project:

### Category A — Improvable Without Changing the Truth

These are findings where the candidate *already has* the relevant experience,
but the resume doesn't surface it effectively. Examples:

- A skill is mentioned in passing but not highlighted against a key JD requirement
- A bullet point is vague where the JD asks for specifics the candidate actually has
- Relevant experience is buried or poorly ordered
- Terminology doesn't match the JD's language, but the underlying work is the same

**Category A improvements preserve truth. They reorganize, clarify, or re-emphasize —
they never fabricate.**

### Category B — Real Gaps Requiring Actual Experience

These are findings where the JD asks for something the candidate genuinely lacks.
No amount of rewording can fix these. Examples:

- The JD requires 5 years of Kubernetes experience; the candidate has none
- A specific certification is required that the candidate doesn't hold
- The role demands people management experience the candidate hasn't had
- A required technology or domain is completely absent from the resume

**Category B gaps are reported honestly. The system does not attempt to hide,
minimize, or work around them.**

## Who This Is For

- Candidates who want an honest assessment before applying
- Candidates who want to improve their resume without losing their voice
- Career coaches who need a structured gap analysis
- Anyone who values truthfulness over optimization theater
